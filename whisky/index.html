1 · Data sufficiency — red / amber / green

Green = enough verified material to walk in confident. Amber = solid but a gap (usually a second numeric score or a missing market). Red = thin, treat with caution. This is the honest signal of where the research stands.
Criteria: ≥3 reviews · ≥2 numeric scores · UK price · US price · auction/secondary point · a datapoint within the freshness window. ✔ filled / ◌ missing.
