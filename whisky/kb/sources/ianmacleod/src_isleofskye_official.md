Isle of Skye blended Scotch (Ian Macleod), via WebSearch excerpts (isleofskyewhisky.com + award coverage):

- All expressions bottled at 40% ABV. Blend of peated island malt (heart = Talisker, the only distillery on Skye) softened by Speyside + Highland malts and grain whisky; 25YO matured in a combination of ex-bourbon and ex-sherry casks.
