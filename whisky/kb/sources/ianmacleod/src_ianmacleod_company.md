Company facts (cross-confirmed across ianmacleod.com, Wikipedia, scotchwhisky.com via WebSearch excerpts):

- "Ian Macleod Distillers Ltd. is an independent, family-owned business which produces Scotch whisky and other spirits, based in Broxburn, Scotland. It was founded by Leonard Russell, Snr. in 1933."
- "The Russell family acquired Ian Macleod & Co Ltd in March 1963, with Peter Russell as Managing Director. The company remains family-owned and operated by the Russell family today."
- Portfolio (distilleries): Glengoyne (Highland), Tamdhu (Speyside), Rosebank (Lowland), and Edinburgh Gin (gin distillery, Edinburgh).
- Brands: "principal blended brands are Isle of Skye, Smokehead and King Robert"; also Sheep Dip, Pig's Nose, independent bottlings under "As We Get It", Chieftain's and Dun Bheagan banners.
- Acquisitions: Glengoyne purchased from The Edrington Group for £7.2 million in 2003; Tamdhu acquired from Edrington in 2011 (reopened 2012, mothballed since 2009); Rosebank acquired 2017.
- Scale: "produces and sells over 15 million bottles of spirits every year."
