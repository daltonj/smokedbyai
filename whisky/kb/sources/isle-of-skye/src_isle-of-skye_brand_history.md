"In 1933 Ian Macleod created the unique blend of Isle of Skye, using specially selected whisky from Skye and the mainland of Scotland. The Skye-born blender created his secret family recipe by blending peated Island malts with smooth Speyside and Highland malts as well as grain whiskies. Ian Macleod created the brand as an homage to Clan Macleod, which has its home at Dunvegan Castle on the Isle of Skye. ... Talisker is a separate entity from Isle of Skye ... a blended Scotch whisky that may contain malt whiskies from various distilleries, including potentially Talisker ... Isle of Skye is not a single malt from Skye but a blended Scotch from Ian Macleod Distillers, originally created in 1933 and acquired by Ian Macleod & Co in 1963. The brand operates an unusually deep core range that spans from 8 to 30 years old expressions."
