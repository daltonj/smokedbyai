"The 25 Year Old is created from mature whiskies drawn from Skye and mainland Scotland, aged in ex-bourbon and sherry casks, and blended with a high malt content to deliver depth and character. The nose offers rich oak, ginger, vanilla, red apple, and citrus. The palate is rounded and fruity, with malt, apple, and light peat smoke. The finish is long, balanced, with subtle apricot notes. ... Another detailed review describes lush and fragrant earthy notes of Autumn leaves, along with warm baking spices, brown sugar, orchard fruits, toasted walnuts, burnt orange citrus, and hints of buttery toffee on the nose, with alluring complexity and well rounded flavor notes such as hazelnuts, warm spices, blood orange zest, dark wood polish, gentle smoke, a touch of sea salt, and a spot of leather on the palate."
