"Isle of Skye 30 Year Old, produced by Ian Macleod Distillers, earned a Gold medal at the 2026 World Whiskies Awards for its depth, subtlety, and balance. At its heart is Talisker, the only distillery on Skye, whose smoky, maritime character is softened by long-aged malts from Speyside and Highland distilleries. The full blend includes malt and grain whiskies aged at least 30 years, married in oak and bottled at 40% ABV. The result is complex without being heavy, with hints of soft peat, dried fruit, vanilla, and malted biscuit ... The nose boasts lush, earthy notes like forest floor after it rains, along with rich aromas of fragrant red berries, cinnamon, citrus, buttery caramel, and salted pretzels. The palate is vivid in complexity with notes of warm spices, black tea, lemon citrus, vanilla, orchard fruits, and brown sugar, with hints of soft leather and chocolate malted balls."
