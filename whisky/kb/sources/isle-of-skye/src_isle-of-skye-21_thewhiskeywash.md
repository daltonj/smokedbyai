"The Isle of Skye 21-Year-Old is a blend of peated island malts that have been matched with Speyside and Highland malts as well as grain whiskies. It's a 700 ml bottle with an SRP of $114.99.
Appearance: Chestnut/Oloroso sherry. Nose: notes of warm baking spices, red apples, buttery toffee, citrus, and chocolate malted balls. Palate: subtle stone fruit sweetness, with flavors of spicy caramel, black currant, chocolate, lemon zest, and a hint of cedar wood and green peppercorn.
Overall: a smooth textured, easy-sipping scotch blend with a medium to long finish without an over abundance of complexity. Medium-bodied ... a subtly decadent treat of a blended scotch."
